Run a meeting

1

Turn the room on

Touch the panel on the table. If the screen is dark, tap Turn room on. The LED wall shows the room name and your next booking within a few seconds.

2

Start or join a Teams meeting

  • Booked meeting: it appears on the Teams console at the table. Tap Join.
  • Meeting right now: tap Start Teams meeting on the room panel.

The camera only sends video during a meeting. It frames the group and follows whoever is speaking.

3

Share your laptop

  1. Plug the HDMI cable at the table into your laptop (use the adapter ring for USB-C).
  2. Tap Share laptop.

In a meeting, remote people see your screen too. Tap Stop sharing when you're done.

4

Volume and microphones

  • Use − and + at the bottom of the panel for the room speakers.
  • Mute mics turns red when the room can't be heard. Tap it again to talk.

The microphones are in the ceiling above the table. Speak normally; there's no need to lean in.

5

When you finish

  1. Tap Leave meeting.
  2. Tap Turn room off, or just leave: the room turns itself off when it has been empty for a while.
  3. Take your laptop and leave the HDMI cable on the table.

Using both rooms together

+

Combine Lakeview A and B

  1. Ask facilities to open the airwall between the rooms (book the combined room and it's done for you).
  2. When the wall is fully open, the panels ask Combine the rooms? Tap Combine rooms.
  3. Both LED walls, cameras, microphones and speakers now work as one room. Either panel controls everything.

Rooms can't combine while either side is in a meeting, so leave it first. To split again, tap Divide rooms; closing the airwall also splits them.

Something not working?

Remote people can't hear us
  • Check Mute mics on the panel isn't red.
  • Make sure you joined from the room (Teams console), not only from your laptop.
  • If your laptop is also in the call, mute its microphone and speakers to avoid echo.
We can't hear the remote people
  • Press + a few times on the panel.
  • Check the meeting on the Teams console is not muted on their side.
My laptop doesn't show on the screen
  • Check the panel shows HDMI connected under Share laptop. If not, unplug and replug the cable.
  • On Windows press Win + P and choose Duplicate; on a Mac choose Mirror in Displays.
  • Tap Share laptop again.
The rooms won't combine
  • The airwall must be fully open; the panel says Airwall is open when it is.
  • Leave any running meeting on both sides first.
  • If the panel says combining is switched off, the room is in maintenance; contact the help desk.
The LED wall is black
  • Tap Turn room on and wait 10 seconds.
  • Still black? Report it below; the system runs a self-test and sends the result to the technicians.
